          Questions candidates ask

          Who can take this assessment?

          Any college student, fresher or early-career graduate. There are no college tier or percentage cutoffs.

          When is the exam?

          A batch forms once 100 candidates enroll. Exams run on weekends and you pick your slot from your dashboard.

          What if my internet drops?

          Answers autosave on every question. Log back in on the same browser and carry on; the timer stays on our server.

          What does the paper contain?

          100 multiple-choice questions across Quantitative Aptitude, Technical Fundamentals and Logical Reasoning, in 60 minutes.

          When do I get my result and certificate?

          Evaluation is automated. Your score, level and certificate appear on your dashboard within a few minutes of submission.

          Can employers verify my certificate?

          Yes. Every certificate has a unique ID and QR code that recruiters can check against our records.

          Can I retake it for a higher level?

          You can take the test once per drive. Retake windows are announced on your dashboard.
