Parties
Prerna Engineering Educational Group Pvt. Ltd. (“PRERNA”) and Employmentexpress Verband LLP (“E2V”)
Date Signed
14 August 2023
Place of Execution
Noida, Uttar Pradesh
Backing Parties
HDFC Life Insurance Company Ltd. (funder) and NSDC International (implementing body)
Nature
Commercial, milestone-based service agreement (financial obligations attached)
Signatories
Nitesh Kumar (Director, Prerna) and Ankit Sharma (Director, E2V)
Purpose & Scope
  • HDFC Life funds NSDC International under a Principal Agreement; NSDC International implements the programme through associates such as Prerna, who in turn engages E2V as an on-ground partner company.
  • Objective: mobilise and train candidates so they can clear the IRDA (insurance regulatory) exam and be onboarded, as part of a skilling/employability initiative.
E2V's (Partner Company) Obligations
  • Mobilise at least 1,000 candidates — minimum 18 years old, 10th pass; at least 50% must be 25+ years and graduates, with preference to housewives, retired government servants/bankers, or those experienced in BFSI.
  • Ensure mobilised candidates are willing to visit the nearest IRDA exam centre and complete registration on the prescribed digital portal.
  • Conduct at least 10 hours of virtual instructor-led training per candidate, covering IC 38 content clarification/revision and sales-pitch skill-building for insurance products.
  • Submit training completion evidence to the insurance company, conduct 2–3 mock tests, and report training details in the SIP system.
Financials
  • Candidates pay only the IRDAI exam fee of Rs. 504 (18+ age) directly, via an online payment link at registration.
  • E2V is reimbursed Rs. 800 per candidate in milestone-based tranches: Rs. 700 for “Login of PRL without FR” and Rs. 100 for “IRDA Exam Pass & Onboard” (with Training Initiation and Exam Scheduling as reporting milestones).
  • Payment is made only on completion of each defined milestone, based on report formats (Training Report, Coding Report) — no payment for incomplete milestones.
Milestone Definitions
  • Login of PRL without FR: application logged as First Time Right, with no Further Requirements raised at scrutiny.
  • Training Initiation: Start Date populated when the candidate begins the first training module.
  • IRDA Exam Scheduling: Exam Date populated once the IRDAI exam is scheduled for the candidate.
  • IRDA Exam Pass: Result Status marked “Pass” in the Coding Report.

Criteria are subject to change at the discretion of HDFC Life / NSDC International.
