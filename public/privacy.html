Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Employment Express Verband LLP and its affiliates (collectively "Employment Express Verband LLP, we, our, us") collect, use, share, protect or otherwise process your information/personal data through our website https://MYLAT.COM (hereinafter referred to as "Platform"). Please note that you may be able to browse certain sections of the Platform without registering with us.

This privacy policy applies to all information collected through our website (mylat.com), and/or any related services, sales, marketing or events (we refer to them collectively in this privacy policy as the "Services").

1Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the Services, express an interest in obtaining information about us or our products and Services, when you participate in activities on the Services (such as taking assessments), or otherwise when you contact us.

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, academic records, resume/CV, government-issued IDs (for verification), and payment information.
  • Assessment Data: Responses to exam questions, code submissions, webcam/audio feeds (during proctored exams), and overall assessment scores (Levels 1 and 2).
  • Usage Data: IP address, browser and device characteristics, operating system, referring URLs, device name, country, location, and information about how and when you use our Services.

2How We Use Your Information

We use personal information collected via our Services for a variety of business purposes described below:

  • To facilitate account creation and logon process.
  • To provide and manage assessments: Ensuring the integrity of our exams through proctoring and verifying candidate identity.
  • To facilitate placement opportunities: Sharing your profile, assessment scores, and resume with our verified hiring partners to connect you with internship and full-time opportunities.
  • To process payments: Managing fee transactions, refunds, and generating invoices.
  • To send administrative information: Sending you details about your exam slots, results, and changes to our terms, conditions, and policies.
  • To protect our Services: Keeping our platform safe and secure (for example, for fraud monitoring and prevention).

3Information Sharing and Disclosure

We may share your data with third parties in the following situations:

  • Hiring Partners: With your consent, we share your assessment results, profile details, and contact information with verified hiring partners to assist in your job placement.
  • Service Providers: We may share your data with third-party vendors, service providers, contractors, or agents who perform services for us or on our behalf and require access to such information to do that work (e.g., payment processing, data analysis, email delivery, hosting services, and proctoring services).
  • Legal Obligations: We may disclose your information where we are legally required to do so in order to comply with applicable law, governmental requests, a judicial proceeding, court order, or legal process.

4Cookies and Similar Technologies

We may use cookies and similar tracking technologies (like web beacons and pixels) to access or store information. Specific information about how we use such technologies and how you can refuse certain cookies is set out in our Cookie Notice.

5Data Retention and Security

We will only keep your personal information for as long as it is necessary for the purposes set out in this privacy notice, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. We have implemented appropriate technical and organizational security measures designed to protect the security of any personal information we process. However, despite our safeguards and efforts to secure your information, no electronic transmission over the Internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

6Your Privacy Rights

In some regions, you have certain rights under applicable data protection laws. These may include the right (i) to request access and obtain a copy of your personal information, (ii) to request rectification or erasure; (iii) to restrict the processing of your personal information; and (iv) if applicable, to data portability. You can manage your preferences by logging into your candidate dashboard or contacting our support team.

7Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy notice from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by an updated "Last updated" date and the updated version will be effective as soon as it is accessible. We encourage you to review this privacy notice frequently to be informed of how we are protecting your information.
