06 · Your questions

Questions candidates ask

Have questions before you register? Everything you need to know about the exam and placement process.

Any college student, fresher, or early-career graduate registered on InternBoot. There are no strict college tier or percentage cutoffs — everyone is evaluated purely on demonstrated aptitude and skill.

A batch is formed once 100 candidates enroll. Exams are held every weekend (Saturday and Sunday). Once your batch is formed, you can pick your preferred timing slot directly from your candidate dashboard.

The test contains 100 multiple-choice questions across Quantitative Aptitude, Technical Fundamentals, and Logical Reasoning, completed in 60 minutes.

Your responses are autosaved on every question. If your connection drops, simply refresh or log back in on the same browser to resume right where you left off. The exam timer is securely maintained on our server.

Evaluation is automated. Your score, Level assignment, and verifiable certificate are generated within a few minutes of submission on your candidate dashboard.
